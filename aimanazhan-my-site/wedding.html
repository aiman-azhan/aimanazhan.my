The Nikah

Aiman & Dee

A garden resort in Kuala Lumpur, everything in white, and the people we love most. This is how the day went.

It started quietly. The dress waited on its hanger, the rings sat in their box, and Dee took her time getting ready.

Her bridesmaids were never far away. In soft grey, they fixed a veil here and a hem there, and laughed the whole way through.

On the other side of the resort, I was with my groomsmen. Songkok on, hantaran trays in hand, and a few nerves to walk off in the garden.

Then the akad. The words were said once, clearly, and the whole room let out its breath. Mine took a little longer to come back.

We walked in together for the first time as husband and wife: under the green arch, down the aisle and onto the pelamin.

Everyone we love was in one room that night. Family, old friends and colleagues, all dressed in white.

The night ended with fireworks, and then a quiet minute to ourselves by the water.

To everyone who came, helped, prayed and danced.
