Five agents, one team

The team

Each agent owns a whole job, not a single task. It reads the customer history, decides the next step within the rules, and passes the work on.

1

Marketing agent

Hands new leads to Sales →

Drafts campaigns in the business's tone and sends them to opted-in contacts after approval. Files every reply and sign-up.

2

Sales agent

Hands won deals to Operations →

Responds to new enquiries within a minute, in English or Bahasa Malaysia. Asks qualifying questions and follows up on quotes until they are won or lost.

3

Operations agent

Hands open orders to Finance →

Turns a won deal or completed form into a numbered sales order and confirmation, in the format the customer needs, within the same hour.

4

Finance agent

Flags overdue accounts →

Follows up on unpaid orders on a set schedule and passes settled orders to the accounting software, leaving the e-Invoice setup unchanged.

5

Support agent

Hands hard questions to a person →

Answers customer questions on WhatsApp and email at any hour, using the business's policies and order records. Phone calls go to the AI Voice Receptionist.

Example

A training provider runs a public course. Each agent picks up where the last one stopped. Follow the green.

  1. 1

    Marketing

    The campaign is approved and goes to past participants who opted in.

  2. 2

    Sales

    A reader asks about group rates, gets an answer within a minute and signs up.

  3. 3

    Operations

    The sign-up form becomes a numbered sales order for the participant's company.

  4. 4

    Finance

    The unpaid order is chased until settled, then handed to accounting.

  5. 5

    Support

    Venue, parking and certificate questions are answered on WhatsApp before the course.

Ground rules

People approve

Every campaign, and anything outside the set rules, waits for a person.

Says it is automated

Agents disclose that they are automated and hand difficult questions to a person.

Opted-in only

Only opted-in contacts receive email, and every email has a way to unsubscribe.

Everything logged

Every action is recorded in the CRM.

Invoices stay put

Invoices remain in the accounting software.
